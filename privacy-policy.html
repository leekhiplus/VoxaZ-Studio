Privacy Policy

VoxaZ Studio · Effective October 17, 2026

This policy explains how VoxaZ Studio (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The app is published by {{DEVELOPER}}. Questions: {{EMAIL}}.

1. The short version

  • VoxaZ Studio is a video player. It plays videos stored on your device and network streams that you choose to open.
  • There is no account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your videos are never uploaded to us. What the app remembers stays on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob (see section 5).

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Photos and videos / storage (read-only): to list and play the videos on your device. Older Android versions call this “storage”.
  • Internet: to play network streams you enter, and to load ads.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly the app saves the following on your device only: your favorites, playlist, watch history, the position where you stopped each video, your settings, and the PIN of the optional Child lock. None of it is sent to us. You can clear the watch history in Settings, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

4. Network streams

When you open a stream URL, your device connects directly to the server that hosts it. That server may receive your IP address and request details and is governed by its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store the addresses you open.

5. Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. This is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you will be asked for your consent. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional Child lock lets a parent restrict the Shorts feed to scrolling only; it does not collect any information.

7. Security

The app has no user accounts or servers of its own, so your data is not stored by us. We take reasonable care, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices

  • Revoke the video permission at any time in your phone’s app settings.
  • Clear the watch history in Settings, or clear the app’s data.
  • Manage ad personalization in your Android settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
